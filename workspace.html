What the review checks

Hook & first frame

Does the opening make a clear promise or create curiosity?

Visual clarity

Composition, visible subject and obvious visual distractions in sampled frames.

Curiosity & payoff

Whether notes and sampled frames suggest a story that leads somewhere.

Caption and SEO

Relevant caption, natural keywords, and hashtags that match the actual clip.

Frame-sampled AI review is not a measured retention graph. Actual watch time and retention must come from platform analytics.
