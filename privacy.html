Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Luna Social Manager ("we", "us", or "our") helps users manage original social-media content through authorized platform APIs. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the service.

Information we process

  • Account information: information returned by a social platform after you authorize our application, such as a platform user ID, username, profile information, and permitted account statistics.
  • Content information: videos, captions, cover information, publishing status, and related metadata that you choose to submit through the service.
  • Technical information: limited information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

How we use information

We use authorized information only to provide the requested creator-management functions, including connecting an account, preparing or publishing content where the platform permits it, checking publishing status, and troubleshooting requests.

Social-platform permissions

When you connect a social account, the platform shows you the permissions requested by the application. We only request permissions needed for the functions we intend to provide. You can revoke an authorization through the relevant platform's account or app settings.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the application, or by the social platform itself when you authorize an action such as publishing content. We do not disclose information for unrelated advertising purposes.

Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Data deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Luna Social Manager. To request deletion, contact us through the Contact page and identify the account or information you want deleted. We may need to verify the request before completing it. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Luna Social Manager is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use our Contact page.
