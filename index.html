Luna Social Manager

A lightweight social-media management tool for organizing and publishing original short-form video content and viewing supported creator information.

What we do

Luna Social Manager is designed to help a creator manage original content, connect supported social accounts through their authorized APIs, publish content when permitted, and review basic account or publishing information.

Users authorize their own social accounts. We do not ask for or store social-media passwords.
