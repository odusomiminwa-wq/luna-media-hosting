02 / The LUNA rating framework

A repeatable checklist inspired by short-form creator analytics—not a copy of another site's proprietary system.

01 · First-frame stop power

Is the first frame visually clear, distinctive, and immediately interesting?

02 · Hook in 1–2 seconds

Does the opening create a question, tension, desire, or unexpected visual?

03 · Curiosity & pacing

Does each beat add information instead of repeating the same pose or shot?

04 · Payoff / twist

Is there a clear reveal, emotional truth, or satisfying ending?

05 · Caption & audience action

Does the caption match the actual video and invite a natural comment, share, or follow?

03 / What to learn from real results

Use platform analytics after posting. A score is a planning aid, not a guarantee of virality.

Record the baseline

Views, average watch time, completion/retention if available, shares, saves, comments, and follows.

Find the drop-off

Compare the opening and story beats with retention data when the platform provides it.

Change one thing

Test a different first frame, shorter setup, stronger reveal, or clearer caption—not everything at once.

Keep what works

Record the result and reuse the winning creative pattern in a new original video.
